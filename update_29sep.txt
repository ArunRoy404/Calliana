We are pleased to share the next update on the project. Development is
progressing on schedule, and the Agents and Clients sections of the admin
area are now in place.

Today we would like to share a visual update of the website:

 - We have refined the Admin Dashboard: the top bar now stays in place while
   you scroll, the side menu is slimmer and cleaner, and each section of the
   page now appears smoothly one after another as it loads
 - We have developed the Agents page, listing every agent with their call
   status, Zoiper connection, assigned clients, calls today and last activity
 - We have added search, an availability filter and a choice of how many rows
   to show per page, along with page-by-page navigation
 - We have developed the "Add New Agent" panel, including role, availability,
   client assignment, call queue and working hours, with checks that let you
   know if anything is missing or incorrect
 - We have developed the Agent Details panel, with Profile, Performance,
   Assigned Clients, Calls, Tasks, Schedule and Activities tabs
 - We have developed the Clients page, listing every client with their
   primary contact, phone, last interaction, upcoming appointments, open tasks
   and status
 - We have added search, category and status filters, and the same
   rows-per-page choice and page navigation as the Agents page
 - We have developed the "Add New Client Account" panel, covering business
   information, primary contact, agent assignment, status and support notes
 - We have developed the Client Details page, with Overview, Contacts, Calls,
   Messages, Appointments, Tasks, Support Instructions and Activities tabs
 - We have made every page shareable: searches, filters, the page you are on,
   an open panel and the selected tab are all kept in the web address, so
   copying the link and sending it to a colleague opens exactly the same view
 - We have made every new screen fully responsive; on phones and tablets the
   Agents and Clients lists switch to easy-to-read cards

Please note that the screens are currently presented with sample content and
are not yet connected to live data. Any button that is not yet wired to the
backend will confirm this on screen, so nothing appears broken while you
review.
